Soham Papers Pvt Ltd is described on its PaperIndex profile as a duplex board manufacturer based in Gujarat, India, and a manufacturer and exporter.

Company overview

Quality at a
reasonable cost.

The company profile states that Soham Papers manufactures different types of coated and uncoated duplex board papers in different GSM, from 180 to 450. Products are supplied in reel form and are used for making corrugated boxes, liners, textile tubes and duplex cartons, with packaging identified as the main consumption area.

The stated USP is customer satisfaction and good quality at reasonable cost.
